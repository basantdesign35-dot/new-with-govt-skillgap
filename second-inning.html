FAQ

Common questions.

Will I get a job guarantee?

No. SkillGap AI gives guidance and a plan. Hiring decisions are made by employers.

Can serving government employees take private jobs?

Rules differ by service and department. Please check your conduct rules and get the required permission or resignation/VRS clearance before accepting any private role.

Is my profile saved online?

This version saves your profile in this browser, and in our database if it is connected. No password is collected. Use the same browser to return to your dashboard.

Which corporate roles are covered?

Operations, HR, compliance, data/MIS, project coordination, training and facilities/security management. More will be added.
